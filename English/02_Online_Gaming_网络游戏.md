# Online Gaming — For and Against
**Essay · about 150 words · B1–B2**

## English

Online gaming is one of the most popular free-time activities today, especially among young people. Like everything, it has advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, online games connect players from all over the world, so people make friends and practise foreign languages. They also develop teamwork and reflexes, because many games require fast decisions and cooperation. In addition, gaming is entertaining and can be a good way to relax after a long day.

On the other hand, there are serious disadvantages. Some games are addictive, and players can spend hours in front of the screen. They also cost money, because consoles, games and subscriptions are expensive. Finally, gamers do less physical activity, which can lead to health problems such as back pain or poor sleep.

To sum up, online gaming can be fun and even educational, but it must be balanced with sport, study and real-life contact.

## 中文

网络游戏是当今最受欢迎的休闲活动之一，尤其在年轻人中。和一切事物一样，它有利也有弊。

一方面，网络游戏把世界各地的玩家连接起来，人们因此结交朋友、练习外语。它们还能培养团队合作和反应能力，因为很多游戏需要快速决策和配合。此外，游戏很有娱乐性，是漫长一天后放松的好方式。

另一方面，也存在严重的缺点。有些游戏容易上瘾，玩家会在屏幕前花上好几个小时。游戏也很花钱，因为游戏机、游戏和会员订阅都不便宜。最后，玩家的体育活动变少，可能导致背痛或睡眠不好等健康问题。

总而言之，网络游戏可以很有趣，甚至很有教育意义，但必须与运动、学习和现实生活中的交流保持平衡。
